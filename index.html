About SRPow

Power conversion & storage, engineered in-house

SRPow (Shenzhen Resource Power Tech Co., Ltd.) focuses on power conversion and energy storage battery technology, building safe and reliable solutions for off-grid and hybrid energy systems.

We serve homes, commercial & industrial users and remote regions with complete energy systems — from hybrid and off-grid inverters to stackable lithium battery packs, backed by our own BMS, EMS and cloud monitoring.
